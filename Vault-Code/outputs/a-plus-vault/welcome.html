FAQ

Good questions.

Short answers before launch. Anything missing? Use the privacy contact on the legal page.

Can I use it today?

Yes, in beta. The web app (on your computer or phone) and the Chrome extension are ready to try: log in and start keeping. Some things are still rough, so tell us what you find. The Android and desktop apps are coming soon and will launch together with the web app.

Is it free?

It is free during the beta. If paid plans arrive later, you will be told well before anything changes.

Is my Vault private?

Yes. Everything you keep is private by default. Sharing is always your choice and can be turned off again.

What is the Museum?

A browsable collection of open-access works from museums, shown the way a designer reads them: colors, composition, pattern. You can keep any piece into your Vault in one tap. The Museum is where you look around; My Vault is where your own references live.

Where do the Museum images come from?

Only open-licence sources such as CC0 and public-domain collections, each with its credit. Images with unknown rights never appear in the Museum.

Can I search in Thai?

Yes. Search by a few Thai or English words, by colour, or by a similar image.

Which devices does it work on?

Any browser today. You can install it on your phone, and the Android and desktop apps are coming soon.
