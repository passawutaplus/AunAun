Privacy

It only acts
when you do.

    What it does

  • Saves only after you choose + Keep, Snapshot, or Keep in Vault
  • Sends the page URL and title, the image/video/link you picked, and your note
  • Stores your token and recent previews in Chrome’s local storage on your device

    What it never does

  • No background browsing-history collection
  • No hidden page scraping
  • No database secrets inside the extension
  • No automatic publishing of what you keep
Questions

Good to know.

Why isn’t it on the Chrome Web Store yet?

We’re finishing the alpha first. Once it’s listed, this page will link straight to the store and you’ll get automatic updates. Until then, loading it yourself takes about two minutes.

Will it work in Edge, Brave, or Arc?

It’s built as a standard Chrome (Manifest V3) extension, so other Chromium browsers can usually load it the same way. We test on Chrome first.

Why can’t I just click “Add to Chrome”?

Chrome only offers one-click installs for extensions listed on the Chrome Web Store. We’re preparing that listing — until then, the three steps above are the way in. After that, updating is the only manual part.

How do I update it?

Download the newest .zip, replace the folder you loaded, and press the reload icon on the extension in chrome://extensions.

How does it connect without a token?

When you’re logged in to A+ Vault in the same browser, the web app hands a private sync key straight to the extension on that page — nothing to copy or paste. You can still paste a token manually in the popup if you prefer.

Can anyone see what I keep?

No. Your Vault is private by default. The sync key only lets the extension save into your own account, and you can refresh it from Settings any time.

What can I keep?

Images, videos, links, selected text or URLs, whole pages, and region snapshots. Each keeps its source so you can find it — and credit it — later.
