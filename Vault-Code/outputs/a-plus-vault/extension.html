(05)Privacy

It only acts
when you do.

What it does

  • Saves only after you choose + Keep, Snapshot, or Keep in Vault
  • Sends the page URL and title, what you picked, and your note
  • Keeps your key and recent previews in Chrome’s local storage, on your device

What it never does

  • Collect your browsing history in the background
  • Scrape pages you didn’t pick
  • Hold database secrets inside the extension
  • Publish anything you keep
(06)Questions

Good
to know.

Why can’t I just click “Add to Chrome”?

Chrome only offers one-click installs for extensions listed on the Chrome Web Store. We’re preparing that listing. Until then the three steps above are the way in — and after that, updating is the only manual part.

How does it connect without a token?

When you’re logged in to A+ Vault in the same browser, the web app hands a private sync key straight to the extension on that page. Nothing to copy or paste. You can still paste a token in the panel if you prefer.

Will it work in Edge, Brave, or Arc?

It’s a standard Chrome (Manifest V3) extension, so other Chromium browsers can usually load it the same way. We test on Chrome first.

How do I update it?

Download the newest .zip, replace the folder you loaded, and press the reload icon on the extension in chrome://extensions.

Can anyone see what I keep?

No. Your Vault is private by default. The sync key only lets the extension save into your own account, and you can refresh it from Settings any time.

What can I keep?

Images, videos, links, selected text or URLs, whole pages, and region snapshots. Each keeps its source so you can find it — and credit it — later.
