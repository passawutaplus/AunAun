# Brand direction (draft v1, from the owner's answers of 2026-10-04)
Status: DRAFT. Name is pending (see Name). Everything here is owner-approved wording or a proposal marked as such.

## Who and why
- User: freelance designer (all disciplines). Opens Discover to find ideas, keeps images "in the bag", later searches them.
- Problem: saved images get lost or forgotten ("why did I save this?"); other sites do not solve it.
- Role: designer's assistant. Core difference: turns an image into usable data (colors, tags, concept) so it can be searched and referenced, with a link back to the original and credit.
- Look: minimal, clean, designer feel, the image is the hero (reference: Cosmos.so). Do not restyle the existing site.

## Promise (only what can be checked)
1. What you keep stays kept (nothing disappears).
2. You can find it fast (search by words, #tag, color).
3. You can go back to the original with credit.
Do NOT promise: "no ads" (see Ads), accuracy of AI tags, that we find inspiration for every query, any right to reuse an image.
Safe privacy promise at all times: "Your private Vault is never used to target ads."

## Perspective
Great work builds on what already exists. Keep the "what exists" well.

## Vision (proposal; owner unsure)
Every designer has their own organized, searchable library of inspiration, ready in seconds.

## Attitude and voice
Encouraging, supportive, creative, curious about new ideas, quiet. Never boasts (no "best", no "#1", no "AI-powered" bragging). Thai primarily for UX copy; English leads the brand name and taglines to reach international users. Never discourages or belittles anyone's work. Be honest: do not say "safe" or "never lost" unless tested.
Voice examples (owner draft, kept):
- Saved: "Kept. Explore another, and see you in My Vault."

## Tagline and slogan
- Tagline (recommended): **Keep what inspires you.** TH: เก็บทุกแรงบันดาลใจ ไม่ให้หาย
- Slogan / CTA (owner's own, strongest): **+Keep in Vault**
- Alternates: "Find it. Keep it." · "Your inspiration, kept." · "Images, made useful."
- Avoid: "We find inspiration for you" (the feed starts with open-access museum images only).

## Value line (replaces "ready to use right away")
EN: Every image comes with its colors, tags and concept, ready to search and reuse.
TH: ทุกภาพมาพร้อมสี แท็ก และคอนเซ็ปต์ ค้นหาและหยิบไปใช้ต่อได้ทันที
"Reuse" = the data. The image stays the owner's.

## Values that do not bend
Credit the owner of the work · never present others' work as yours · never belittle work.

## Ads (plan, not a promise)
Launch: no ads. After about 10k users the owner may add contextual sponsored placements (design tools, fonts, courses) chosen by the page's category, no tracking. Doing so reopens phase 12 (consent, cookie notice, policy_version). Product/legal text says "currently no ads", never "never".

Own Vault search: EN "Haven't kept this one yet. Try a color, a mood or a #tag." TH "ยังไม่เคยเก็บอันนี้ไว้ ลองค้นด้วยสี อารมณ์ หรือ #แท็กดูนะ"

Link under it: EN "Tell us what you're looking for →" TH "บอกเราว่าอยากเจออะไร →" (LINE @ to be filled; if answers are stored, list it in the privacy notice).
